<p><b>ZukuLock</b> is a lightweight Android application that locks your screen with a single tap. The app utilizes Shizuku to execute the <code>input keyevent 223</code> (<code>KEYCODE_SLEEP</code>) command, turning the screen off instantly and never waking the device. This is particularly useful for reducing wear and tear on your power button or adding convenience to screen control.</p><p>This app was initially designed for <b>Google Pixel devices</b>. Most screen lock apps require <b>Accessibility permissions</b>, but enabling Accessibility can lead to certain banking apps in Vietnam (and possibly others globally) blocking access for security reasons. <b>ZukuLock</b> offers a workaround by avoiding Accessibility permissions, allowing you to use banking apps seamlessly.</p><p><br><b>Key Features</b></p><ul><li><b>One-Tap Screen Lock:</b> Turns the screen off instantly — it never wakes the device.</li><li><b>No Accidental Camera Launches:</b> Uses <code>KEYCODE_SLEEP</code> (223) instead of <code>KEYCODE_POWER</code> (26), so rapid taps are never interpreted as "double-press power". A 2-second debounce provides an extra layer of protection.</li><li><b>Shizuku Integration:</b> Leverages system-level permissions without requiring root access.</li><li><b>No UI Interruption:</b> Simply tap the app icon to execute the action without launching any additional windows.</li><li><b>Customizable Gestures:</b> Works with launchers like Lawnchair or Nova Launcher to enable double-tap gestures for locking the screen.</li></ul>
